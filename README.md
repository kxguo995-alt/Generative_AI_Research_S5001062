1. Storage and Accessibility: Explain why it is important to store and organise your files efficiently.

Answer: Research files should be organised clearly so that team members can find them easily. Good organisation can also reduce the risk of losing files or using the wrong version.

2.File Storage Methods: Specify which data types may require special access controls. Explain your reasons (2-3sentences)

Answer: Sensitive data such as consent forms and interview transcripts should have restricted access. This is because they may contain personal or confidential information. Other research files can be shared with authorised team members.

3.Why is it important to have a good naming convention?

Answer: A good naming convention makes files easier to identify and find. It also helps team members understand the content of each file and reduces confusion between different versions.
